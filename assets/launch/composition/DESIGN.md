# ETHERGLASS film design

## Style prompt
A warm editorial research instrument: precise thin rules, large clear type, honest quantitative evidence and generous paper margins. Follow the current product UI, with real current-source captures.

## Colors
- #eeede6 paper
- #f9f8f3 surface
- #252c2b ink
- #a4472a copper emphasis
- #474b97 mixture
- #198a8e recovered target

## Typography
Helvetica Neue / Arial headings and body; Georgia italic hook emphasis; SFMono-Regular / Consolas / monospace for units and labels.

## Motion
0.45-second crossfades; 0.45-second slight upward entrances, then readable holds. Data curves do not morph into invented intermediate numerical states.

## Avoid
No neon, generic particles, fake metrics, arbitrary cochannel-separation claims or unlabeled illustrations. No private local paths on screen. No remote fonts.
