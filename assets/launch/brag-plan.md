# ETHERGLASS launch film

## Planning rubric
1. App: local RF recording observatory; inspect complex I/Q, specify a target prior, reconstruct target/residual and export source-bound evidence.
2. Strongest claim: “The structure beneath the interference.” Treat this as an invitation to inspect evidence, not a universal recovery claim.
3. Visual hook: real overlapping-chirp spectral power field, editorial type, copper instrument marks.
4. Real UI: actual observation desk loaded with the fixed synthetic reference, target and residual selectors, evidence export dialog.
5. Shortest satisfying video: 20 seconds at 1280×720, 30fps.
6. Tone: polished; a warm, precise research instrument. Fast, clean reveals followed by readable holds.
7. Audio: original restrained sine-based ambient bed; no narration. It is a composed soundtrack, not RF demodulation.
8. Share caption: ETHERGLASS: inspect a recording, test a target hypothesis, and carry the evidence with you. Local RF analysis with explicit priors, retained residuals, and source-bound exports.
9. Flow: import/reference capture → specify center and symbol-rate prior → target/residual → export evidence.

## Visual identity
Paper #eeede6; surface #f9f8f3; ink #252c2b; copper #a4472a; indigo #474b97; teal #198a8e. Helvetica Neue/Arial, Georgia italic, monospace instrumentation. Source UI sets the identity. No stock illustrations, glowing dashboards or fabricated traces.

## Storyboard
- 0–3 seconds: “The structure beneath the interference.” Real spectral field at right. Brand mark above, synthetic-reference label below. Copper rule introduces motion.
- 3–9 seconds: actual working observation desk. Clean crossfade. Stage captions: “Import a recording” then “Specify the target prior”. Reference capture / complex I/Q, 125 kbaud prior. No fake click or implied automated inference.
- 9–16 seconds: “Recover a target. Keep the residual.” Three aligned spectral plots use the exact fixed reference analysis arrays. Metric: +21.7 dB reference SI-SDR improvement. Qualifier holds throughout: “Single synthetic reference demonstration”. This is not a benchmark or a generalized superiority claim. No claim about blind arbitrary cochannel separation.
- 16–20 seconds: actual export dialog and short “Take the evidence with you.” Brand and github.com/nazeeh111/ETHERGLASS. Source / target / residual / analysis report.

All four scene durations sum to 20 seconds. Primary transition is a 0.45-second crossfade. Entrances finish quickly, content holds. No narration, no voice cloning.

## Audio and cue guidance
Original generated ambient score, not recorded radio. Quiet two-note harmonic texture; soft hand-built sine accents at 3, 9, and 16 seconds; final one-second fade. No bundled music or SFX. Cues are authored directly in the generator; no external cue detection required. No audio-reactive graphics because displayed graphs are real analytical evidence.
